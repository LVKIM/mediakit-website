01 / Why now

Korea is the present.

You think Korea is the next market. It is the current one.

Nexus8234 is the corridor between Spain and Korea. Country code 34 and country code 82. We run four services as a single connected practice: innovation trips, an accelerator, proofs of concept, and advisory.

02 / Our services

Four services. One bridge.

Together, they compound. A corporate that starts with a trip can progress into a proof-of-concept, into an acceleration cohort, into long-term advisory.

01 Innovation Trips

Nexus Xplore.

The room is the product.

Five-day curated delegations to Seoul. Twenty executives per edition. Institutional briefings with GDIN and KITA, corporate visits at LG, Hyundai, and Hanwha. The week ends with signed intent. Two editions per year, by application.

02 Accelerator

Nexus Impulse.

Korea rewards residents, not visitors.

A three-month residency in Seoul. We filter startups through Spain's leading accelerators, form a cohort of six to ten, and bring them to Korea for twelve weeks of corporate access and deal execution. The outcome is a signed JV, PoC or partnership, not a certificate.

03 Proofs of Concept

Nexus Lab.

Validation before commitment.

A structured PoC framework in both directions. Korean companies validating European product-market fit with a Spanish corporate. Spanish startups running PoCs in Korean industrial environments. Medtech, space tech, cybersecurity. Six to twelve weeks. CDTI and KIAT co-funding pathways.

04 Advisory

Nexus Strategy.

Long-term engagements for the corridor.

Multi-quarter advisory for institutions, corporates, and funds building a real presence across Spain and Korea. Strategy, representation, partner development, ongoing operations. Senior operators in both Madrid and Seoul. Quarterly retainer.
